2\. Average geometry of three-dimensional fragmentation mosaics (rock fracture)

***Open:*** Domokos and Lángi conjecture that every normal, face-to-face convex mosaic in R³ has harmonic degree h̄ = n̄v̄/(n̄ + v̄) in the interval (3, 4\]. Here n̄ is the mean number of vertices per cell and v̄ the mean number of cells meeting at a vertex.

- Every value in (3, 4\] is known to be attained.

- The best proven lower bound is only 28/13 ≈ 2.15.

- They also ask whether the set of attainable (n̄, v̄) pairs is bounded.

A companion problem is Domokos’s 2015 conjecture on abrasion. It says that under curvature-driven abrasion flows, the number of static equilibria of a convex body, measured from its centroid, decreases monotonically.

***To do:*** prove the (3, 4\] band using Euler-characteristic and averaging identities on the cell complex. Alternatively, search tessellations of the Laguerre and Voronoi type by computer for a counterexample. For the abrasion conjecture, prove monotonicity or construct a flow and body that violate it.
