# Corrections

## 2026-10-27 — errata to rational-laguerre-mosaics 1.1.0-candidate (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23167002)

The archived PDF, the manuscript source, the Zenodo deposit and the tag `v1.1.0-candidate` are unchanged. These errata were confirmed by the Evidence Press archive audit and are also shown at <https://evidencepress.org/releases/rational-laguerre-mosaics/>.

1. Corrected Remark 6.3. The archived text says that “the examples obtained by taking products in Theorem 5.1” contradict the upper endpoint 2^{d−1} of the proposed band. The products of the Theorem 5.1 family do not do this: for m ≥ 5 that family has h < 4, so its products stay below 2^{d−1}. The remark should read: “Applying the product construction from the proof of Theorem 5.1 to the example of Theorem 1.1 (h = 468/115 > 4) also contradicts the upper endpoint 2^{d−1} of the proposed band in every d ≥ 3.”

   The remark's conclusion, that the upper endpoint fails in every d ≥ 3, stands with the corrected example. No theorem, certificate, computation or stated result is affected.
