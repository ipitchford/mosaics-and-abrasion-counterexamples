# Retrospective publication-intake audit — 5 October 2026

This is not a backdated discovery search. Supplied sources and review comparisons were checked at the affected interfaces.

- Domokos–Langi, DOI 10.1080/10586458.2019.1691090: target harmonic-degree band, original notation retained.
- Joswig–Ziegler, arXiv:math/9812033: cubical-polytope construction is inherited; the supplied finite rational instance has exact checking.
- Ziegler, arXiv:math/0208073, revised 2003, section 7: Schlegel replacement in tilings and high-degree context are prior art; section 4 gives the flag inequality used in the regular-class lower bound.
- Ziegler, arXiv:math/0407042: projected products and complexity approaching 16 are inherited inputs, not new polytope phenomena.
- Rybnikov, DOI 10.1007/PL00009434: publisher abstract verifies lifting/weighted-diagram relevance; full-text overlap was not exhaustively audited. No new general lifting theorem is claimed.
- Domokos, arXiv:1308.4779, equations 49–53 and Conjecture 1: expected count and moment-to-sign inference distinguished from deterministic creation. Assumption 2-A is not refuted.
- Damon (1995), DOI 10.1006/jdeq.1995.1019: original local-Morse/heat-equation reference identified in primary Domokos bibliography; publisher full text not retrieved. Used for attribution, not as a load-bearing proof dependency.
- Huang, arXiv:1506.05030v8, Theorems 1.1–1.2, 2.3–2.4 and section 3: closed-manifold fully nonlinear local existence and linear Schauder estimates. Smooth parameter dependence is derived in the added lemma by the Banach implicit-function theorem, not falsely quoted as a separate theorem of that paper.

Bounded search and comparison do not establish priority. Both papers explicitly separate established machinery from claimed conclusions. No external paper was bundled or relicensed.
